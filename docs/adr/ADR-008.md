title: "Resilience through health checks, retries and dead-letter topics"
kind: cross-cutting
context: "No confirmed order may be lost when any component restarts (REQ-010) and\
  \ stock must not go negative under concurrent orders (REQ-011). Consumers may receive\
  \ duplicate or failing events, and services run as separate processes."
decision: "Use Spring Boot Actuator health probes with automatic restarts, consumer\
  \ retries with backoff, dead-letter topics for poison messages, and idempotent handlers\
  \ keyed by orderId so a repeated stock check or reservation does not double-reserve\
  \ or double-release."
alternatives:
- option: Infinite retry without dead-lettering
  rejected_because: A poison message would block the partition and delay all orders.
- option: Exactly-once semantics across services via distributed transactions
  rejected_because: Complex and fragile; idempotent at-least-once processing is simpler
    and sufficient.
consequences: Restarts and redelivery are safe and stock invariants are preserved.
  Dead-letter topics need monitoring and a replay procedure. Every handler needs idempotency
  tests.
